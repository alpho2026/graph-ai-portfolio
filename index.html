About the Course

Practical, academically grounded generative AI for scientists and professionals.

Based at the University of Geneva, Switzerland, The GRAPH Courses program combines academic rigor with practical, industry-relevant skills. The Generative AI for Work & Research bootcamp is an 8-week live cohort training program focused on no-code AI tools for responsible task automation.

The curriculum emphasizes navigating next token prediction, mitigating hallucinations in statistical outputs, automated literature reviews, context engineering, RAG, custom GPT deployment, workflow automation, and responsible presentation of scientific findings.

Live Workshops

Interactive weekly hands-on sessions applying prework concepts to real research scenarios.

Responsible Automation

Emphasis on human-in-the-loop validation, data privacy, and spotting statistical errors.

Capstone Showcase

Collaborative project presentation demonstrating verified, deployed AI workflows.
